Altair Jenkin's Raw/Unformatted Bulk GC Tank Guide
(A refined version is coming soon!)
Make a 240u mera 240u bic jug(see Altair Jenkin's Bulk Pill Guide for a simple method) , insert in 4 98u increments into the tank, dispose of the excess
Make a 240u derma 240u kelo jug(see Altair Jenkin's Bulk Pill Guide for a simple method), insert in 4 98u increments into the tank, dispose of the excess
Insert 120u inap and 120u dylo into jug to make trico, insert in 2 98u increments into the tank, dispose of any excess.
in an empty jug, mix 30u nitro 30u potassium 30u silicon to make 90u dylo, then add 90u carbon and 90u hydrogen to make imi, now add 60u iron and 60u carbon, then add 60u dex from a bottle to make your Imi and dexP mix. There should be 10u imi and 10u dexP in each tank
