Formatting Guide:

* Bullet
[bullet]Text[/bullet]
or
- Text

Makes the line a bullet point


* Center
[center]Text[/center]

Aligns the text to the center


* Monospace
[mono]Text[/mono]

Causes the text to be aligned with Newspaper spacing, ensures the line is filled bound to bound


* Signature
[sign]

Creates a signature line(?)


* Time
[time]

Automatically fills with the current shift time


* Date
[date]

Automatically fills with the current shift date


* Input Field (May not work on every fork)
[field]

Creates an interactive input field
