Megacompact medbag
Two blood bags, one MBT and one KDT, one syringe and one beaker(sippy cup of GC, quick patchup for yourself)
To make:
Take two empty syringes and one of each chem bottle
30u inap and 30u dylo for each bag makes 60u trico in each bag
Then 120u bic and 120u mera in one bag to have your MBT bag
Then 120u Kelo and 120u Dylo in the other bag, thats KDT
Each bag should be an OD of about one injection(30u?)
Ratios is 2:2;1 for KDT and MBT 

To refill the beaker, take one injection from each bloodbag and put it into the beaker
